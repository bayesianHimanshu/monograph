## Model corrections

Each change below corrects the model in response to a real-data finding from H6. None loosens a constraint beyond what the finding requires.

| Version | Change | H6 finding | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0.0 | product-identity | H6: 64% of NDC records carry no RxCUI, so Product (keyed by RxCUI) conformed 19%. | Product is keyed by product NDC; the RxNorm concept is a separate ClinicalDrug (key RxCUI), referenced optionally from Product and from DrugInvolvement (which was Ref[Product]). |
| 1.0.0 | suspect-rule | H6: openFDA follows ICH E2B, which does not record which suspect is primary; 39% of reports failed. | The rule asks for at least one involvement with role primary suspect, secondary suspect or suspect. AdverseEventReport gains source_system (openfda_e2b, faers_qde) so the provenance of roles is explicit. |
| 1.0.0 | recall-number-pattern | H6: 17% of recall numbers use 3 digits in the middle segment (for example D-321-2016). | The middle segment allows 3 or 4 digits. |
| 1.0.0 | report-id-pattern | H6: safety report IDs can carry a -digits version suffix (for example 5801206-7). | An optional -digits suffix (1 to 3 digits) is allowed. |
| 1.0.0 | regex-digit-escape | H6: 10 of 12 developer-written patterns fell outside the safe subset, most because of \d. | safe() canonicalizes \d to [0-9] for every target, Pydantic included; lookarounds and other constructs are still rejected. |
| 1.0.0 | safe-strings | H6: pySHACL accepts a trailing newline before $, and Postgres cannot store NUL. | Every string is SafeIdentifier (no control characters) or SafeText (no control characters except tab, LF, CR), enforced in Pydantic, SHACL (sh:not) and a Postgres CHECK. Bounded patterns also emit sh:maxLength. |
| 1.0.0 | uniqueness-in-postgres | H6: the two uniqueness constraints were pydantic_only and unenforced in Postgres. | They stay pydantic_only for SHACL, and the DDL generates UNIQUE constraints for them. |
| 1.0.0 | ddl-profiles | H6: the DDL used deferrable foreign keys only because the integration probe needed them. | The default DDL has plain foreign keys; a test profile keeps them deferrable. Both are drift-checked. |
| 2.0.0 | route-conditional | H6.1: 86 of 200 NDC products had no route; 84 of them are not finished (60 bulk ingredients, 23 drugs for further processing, 1 licensed vaccine bulk intermediate) and 2 are finished kits. | Product gains product_type (NDC directory product type) and finished. Routes are required only when the product is finished and its type is human prescription or human OTC drug (a conditional rule); kits are not exempt. |
| 2.0.0 | route-vocabulary | H6.1: the route enum was the 23 routes observed in one sample; real records used routes outside it. | Route is the FDA SPL route of administration list (121 terms with NCI codes). OTIC is replaced by the FDA term AURICULAR (OTIC). |
