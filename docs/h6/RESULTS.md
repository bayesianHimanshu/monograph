## What this means for the PoV

**Verdict: H6 partly holds.** Rule: it holds if every mutation is caught by its expected gate with no false alarm, Pydantic and SHACL agree on every fixture, property case and real record, the round trip is lossless, and the leak inventory is empty. It partly holds if everything but the last condition is met. Otherwise it fails.

* Sync between the one source and its projections is enforced: 100.0% of mutations were caught by the intended gate, false alarms: 0.
* Pydantic and SHACL reach the same verdict: fixtures 100.0%, property cases 100.0%, real records 100.0%. Round trip: 100.0%.
* It holds only inside a projectable subset. Leaks: 3 pydantic_only constraints, 6 constraints the DDL cannot express, 5 invalid fixtures Postgres accepted through implicit casts, 10 of 12 idiomatic regexes outside the safe subset.
* Agreement also depends on the validator implementation: 2 pySHACL deviations from the SHACL spec were observed, and 3 dialect probes disagree (Pydantic and SHACL read the same pattern differently).
* Lowest real-data conformance: Product 19.1%, PackagedProduct 23.4%, AdverseEventReport 61.2%. Pydantic and SHACL judged every real record identically, so these failures are about the source data and the model (keys, enums, rules), not about drift between artifacts.
